In this project, we want to study how a Schwarzschild type reflective objective focus a gaussian beam onto the focal plane. 
Specifically, we look at Thorlabs LMM40X-P01 model (
    https://www.thorlabs.com/reflective-microscope-objectives?pn=LMM40X-P01&tabName=Overview
    https://www.thorlabs.com/item/LMM40X-P01
), and look for the intensity/phase distributions of the spots of a HeNe laser and a 2923 nm laser with various beam diameter.
Finally we want to know the optimal gaussion parameters to fit the spot

"test.ipynb" has some preliminary results from poppy package, which is not necessarily correct.
You should first figure out how the Schwarzschild design works, what is Thorlabs LMM40X-P01 model like, 
and what the right way it is to simulate the beam propogation. And then write you own script.
